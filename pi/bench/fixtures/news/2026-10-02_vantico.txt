Vantico dashboard outage resolved after 47 minutes, about 210,000 users affected

LISBON, October 2, 2026 — Project-management tool vendor Vantico recovered from a dashboard outage on Friday that lasted 47 minutes and affected about 210,000 users, mostly in Iberia and Brazil. API endpoints stayed up throughout, the company said.

The incident was traced to a failed certificate rotation in the front-end gateway. Vantico offered affected workspaces a 30-day credit as an apology. Unlike the larger Pulsegrid outage later in the month, no regulators were involved.
