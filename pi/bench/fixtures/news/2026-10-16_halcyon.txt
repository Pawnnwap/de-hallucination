Tesserion to acquire Halcyon for $1.24 billion in cash and stock

NEW YORK, October 16, 2026 — Enterprise search firm Tesserion said Friday it will acquire observability startup Halcyon Systems for $1.24 billion in cash and stock, its largest deal to date. The price represents a 19% premium over Halcyon's last private valuation of $1.04 billion from January.

Halcyon posted $96 million in annual recurring revenue and was growing 70% a year, according to an investor deck reviewed by this publication. The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, pending antitrust review in the US and the EU.

Halcyon co-founder and CEO Dana Whitfield will lead Tesserion's new Observability division, which the companies say will reach a combined $210 million in bookings next year.
