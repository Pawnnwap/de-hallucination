Nordvane closes $84M Series C led by Marlow Ventures

STOCKHOLM, October 9, 2026 — Wind-fleet analytics company Nordvane said Friday it has closed an $84 million Series C round led by Marlow Ventures, bringing its total funding to $146 million. The round includes a $12 million secondary tranche for early employees.

CEO Anders Lindqvist said the money funds expansion into offshore wind inspection drones, with a first contract signed with utility giant Nordsjo Kraft. Nordvane expects to reach 400 offshore turbines under monitoring by Q3 2027, up from 95 today.

The company declined to disclose valuation but said revenue grew 85% to $31 million in the trailing twelve months.
