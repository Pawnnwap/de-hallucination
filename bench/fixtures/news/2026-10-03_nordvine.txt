Nordvine raises $48M Series B to expand industrial robotics fleet

HELSINKI, October 3, 2026 — Industrial robotics startup Nordvine announced on Saturday it has raised a $48 million Series B round led by Copperline Capital, with participation from existing backers Frostfall and Aalto Growth. The round values the company at $310 million post-money.

Chief Executive Maarit Vainio said the funding will pay for doubling its leased robot fleet to 2,400 units across Nordic warehouses by the end of 2027. "We do not sell robots, we sell uptime," Vainio said in the announcement.

Nordvine reported annual recurring revenue of $19 million, up 140% year over year, and says it is not yet profitable. The company employs 140 staff in Helsinki and Tallinn.
