Nordline secures $48.5M Series B for cold-chain logistics tracking

OSLO, September 28, 2026 — Cold-chain logistics tracker Nordline has raised a $48.5 million Series B led by Copperline Capital, the same firm that backed its $15 million Series A in 2024. The round values Nordline at $220 million.

Nordline's sensors monitor refrigerated truck and ship cargo in real time; the company says its devices tracked 1.2 million pallets last quarter across 14 countries. CEO Ingrid Solheim said the funds will go toward a new decoding chip family and expansion into Japan, planned for the first half of 2027.

The company is profitable on an EBITDA basis since Q4 2025, according to the announcement.
